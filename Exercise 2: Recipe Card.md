# Egg sandwich with ketchup recipe

## Ingredients

-2 eggs

-2 slices of bread 

-ketchup

-cooking oil

1. **Fry the Egg**: Melt a small pat of butter in a frying pan over medium heat. Crack the egg directly into the pan. Poke the yolk with a spatula if you want it cooked flat, and let it cook for 2 minutes.

2. **Flip and Toast**: Flip the egg over. Immediately place your two slices of bread directly into the pan right next to the egg so they can soak up the remaining butter and toast while the other side of the egg cooks (about 1 more minute).

3. **Assemble**: Turn off the heat. Put the egg on one slice of bread, squirt your ketchup right on top of the egg, and cover it with the second slice of bread.
